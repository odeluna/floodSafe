# FloodSafe

Current flood alerts tell people what is happening. FloodSafe helps people decide what to do next.

## Overview

FloodSafe is a flood-awareness and decision-support platform designed to help users better understand flood conditions in their area. By combining flood datasets, dynamic risk visualizations, and an AI-powered assistant, FloodSafe transforms complex flood information into accessible, actionable insights.

## The Problem

During flooding events:
- Traditional navigation tools are not designed around flood awareness.
- Flood-related information is often difficult to interpret.
- Communities need clearer guidance when assessing local flood risks.

FloodSafe addresses these challenges through interactive flood-risk mapping and AI-assisted explanations.

## Key Features

### Dynamic Flood Risk Overlays
FloodSafe visualizes flood-prone areas using provided flood datasets and risk indicators. Users can quickly identify:
- Safer areas
- Higher-risk zones
- Local flood conditions

### Rain AI Assistant
Rain AI analyzes flood-related data and helps users understand local conditions. Rain AI can:
- Explain flood risks
- Analyze potentially hazardous areas
- Provide additional context about affected locations
- Display related roadway imagery for affected areas

### Interactive Flood Visualization
FloodSafe presents flood information through an interactive map-based interface that allows users to explore flood conditions more intuitively than traditional data tables or reports.
